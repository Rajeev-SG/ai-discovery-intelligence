# Consumer surface scope

## Inclusion rule

Track a surface when it is consumer-accessible and can materially influence discovery/recommendation of external brands, products, services, publishers or places. Include globally large products and regionally dominant products even when English-language coverage is sparse.

Do not confuse **model families** with **consumer surfaces**. Retrieval behaviour can differ across ChatGPT, API models, search modes, shopping modes and embedded interfaces using related models.

## Surface types

- conversational assistant;
- AI-native search;
- search augmentation / AI answer layer;
- embedded ecosystem assistant;
- commerce-native AI discovery surface;
- browser-native AI discovery.

## v1 priority families

### Global / cross-market

ChatGPT; Gemini; Google AI Mode; Google AI Overviews; Microsoft Copilot; Bing Copilot Search; Claude; Perplexity; DeepSeek Chat; Grok; Meta AI; Mistral Le Chat; You.com; Duck.ai; Brave AI/Leo; Genspark.

### Mainland China / Chinese ecosystem

Doubao; Qwen/千问; Quark AI; Tencent Yuanbao; Kimi; Baidu AI Search; 360 Nano AI Search; Zhipu/Z.ai Qingyan; Baidu Wenxin Assistant. DeepSeek is already in the global list but must have China-specific audience/methodology evidence too.

### Regionally material

NAVER AI Tab / AI Briefing and Kakao Kanana (Korea); Yandex Search AI / Alice AI and Sber GigaChat (Russia/CIS).

### Watchlist / embedded or vertical

Felo (Japan watch); Manus; Poe; Apple Intelligence/Siri with ChatGPT; Amazon Rufus; other high-scale retailer/marketplace assistants; emerging regional assistants that cross the materiality threshold.

## Why regional inclusion matters

QuestMobile's 2026 H1 research reported roughly 382M MAU for Doubao, 167M for Qwen and 130M for DeepSeek in China. NAVER reported more than 4M cumulative AI Tab users within two months of beta and access from a search ecosystem reaching tens of millions. Yandex reported over 49M monthly users for AI answers in Search. These are not niche surfaces and must not disappear from a Western-centric matrix.
