# Collection, security and legal policy

- Use public/free sources only for v1.
- Respect robots.txt, publisher terms, rate limits and normal access controls.
- Do not evade CAPTCHAs, authentication or anti-bot measures.
- Do not scrape paywalled content into the corpus.
- Store raw evidence privately.
- Public/client-safe output should contain normalized facts, metrics, citations/links and only short source excerpts when necessary.
- Identify vendor-authored research as vendor research.
- Preserve methodology and limitations so claims cannot be detached from context.
- Any automated querying of consumer AI products is a separate later capability and must be reviewed against product terms before implementation.
