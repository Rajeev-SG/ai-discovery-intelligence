# v1 definition

v1 is an internal agency intelligence product for understanding how AI-driven consumer discovery is changing and communicating only material implications to senior media/search leaders.

## v1 must do

- Maintain a broad global registry of commercially relevant consumer AI discovery surfaces.
- Ingest free official documentation, release notes, public research, free industry studies, public feeds and discoverable news/research.
- Normalize reports into evidence-backed claims with dates, regions, sample/methodology and provenance.
- Track conflicts and changing claims over time.
- Present a rich observation table with search, sort, filter, facets and expandable evidence/detail views.
- Score **evidence confidence** separately from **commercial significance**.
- Produce a restrained weekly executive brief containing only material changes.
- Maintain a versioned SEO/AEO/GEO POV and explain exactly why each section changed.
- Show source coverage and gaps so missing evidence is visible.

## v1 must not require

- paid data subscriptions;
- paid Similarweb/Peec/Profound/Semrush/Ahrefs/SISTRIX APIs;
- a home-grown crawler, scheduler, diff engine, workflow orchestrator, table engine or search engine;
- exhaustive prompt-rank tracking for individual client brands;
- automated querying of every consumer chatbot.

## v1 success test

A senior media leader should be able to open the product and answer in minutes:

1. Which AI discovery surfaces matter globally and in a selected market?
2. How does each currently access/search/retrieve the web?
3. What changed materially in the last week/month?
4. How confident are we, and why?
5. What should a brand actually do differently?
6. Which assertions are contested or based on thin evidence?
