# Research baseline — 2026-09-15

This is the seed intelligence used to validate the first product slices. It is not a static truth; the system should supersede it with newer evidence.

## Market fragmentation

Similarweb's May 2026 web data reported ChatGPT at roughly 52.7% of AI-chatbot web traffic, Gemini around 27.3% and Claude around 8.9%, with DeepSeek also generating hundreds of millions of monthly web visits. This makes a ChatGPT-only GEO worldview obsolete.

Source: https://www.similarweb.com/blog/research/market-research/most-visited-websites/

Statcounter's August 2026 AI-chatbot market-share measure differs materially, showing ChatGPT around 79.4%, Gemini 10.9%, Perplexity 4.31%, Copilot 2.79%, Claude 2.57% and DeepSeek 0.02%. This is a useful methodology-normalization test: different measurement systems must not be blended as if they measure the same denominator.

Source: https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share

## China is essential

QuestMobile reported China AI-native app MAU of roughly 382M for Doubao, 167M for Qwen and 130M for DeepSeek in mid-2026.

Source: https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2076954943839809537/

QuestMobile also published a May 2026 study specifically on AI platforms' source-selection logic and source preferences across commercially important categories such as travel, automotive and finance. That is unusually relevant to this product because it goes beyond adoption metrics into how AI changes information distribution and brand source value.

Source: https://www.questmobile.com.cn/research/report/2059118379722096641/

## Regional search-scale AI

Kakao had Kanana in KakaoTalk live by September 2026 and was actively promoting sign-ups; its broader AI strategy places search/recommendation inside a consumer ecosystem with tens of millions of users. Track it separately from NAVER rather than reducing Korea to a single platform.

Source: https://www.kakaocorp.com/page/detail/12132

Baidu consolidated its web AI assistant entry points into Wenxin Assistant in June 2026. Track Wenxin Assistant separately from Baidu AI Search because a conversational assistant and search answer layer can have different retrieval/recommendation behaviour.

Source: https://chat.baidu.com/


NAVER reported its AI Tab exceeding 4M cumulative users within about two months of beta, with product/place card CTR above 20%, and rolling it into a search ecosystem serving tens of millions.

Source: https://www.navercorp.com/media/pressReleasesDetail?seq=10034442

Yandex reported more than 49M monthly users for AI answers inside Search in September 2026.

Source: https://yandex.com/company/news/2026-09-14-01

## Citation/source behaviour is volatile

SISTRIX measured weekly citation churn of roughly 74% for ChatGPT and 56% for Google AI Mode in a 17-week multi-country study. This argues against treating one snapshot/ranking as durable.

Source: https://www.sistrix.com/blog/ai-citation-drift-how-stable-are-sources-in-ai-search-results/

Semrush/Promptwatch reported a sharp mid-August 2026 Reddit citation decline in ChatGPT from about 3.8% to 0.5%, but the source itself said the observation was provisional. Ahrefs' September 2026 dataset subsequently ranked Reddit as ChatGPT's largest cited domain at 16.8% mention share. This should be the canonical conflict-handling acceptance example.

Sources:
- https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/
- https://ahrefs.com/blog/most-cited-domains-in-chatgpt/

## Commercial importance is growing

Similarweb reported average AI-platform referral traffic of roughly 770.7M visits/month across its June 2025–May 2026 dataset, up 117.4% year over year, with strong differences by industry.

Source: https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/ai-referral-traffic-by-industry/

Adobe reported AI-referred retail visitors converting materially better than non-AI traffic in several 2026 datasets; for the June 2026 Prime Day period it reported roughly 40% better conversion among AI-referred shoppers, while noting total AI volume remained modest relative to major channels.

Source: https://business.adobe.com/blog/2026-prime-day-insights

## Free vendor research is valuable but interested

Peec, Profound, Ahrefs, Semrush, SISTRIX, Scrunch and Evertune all publish useful large-sample studies. Their data should be ingested with publisher/methodology metadata and never silently treated as neutral universal truth.
