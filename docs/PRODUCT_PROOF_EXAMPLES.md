# Product-proof examples

These examples define what an issue should demonstrate before closure.

## Observation-plane slice

A browser screenshot/URL shows rows for ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Doubao, NAVER and Yandex. Searching `China` returns China-relevant rows. Sorting by priority works. Expanding DeepSeek shows retrieval unknowns and source links rather than a blank cell.

## Ingestion slice

The system ingests a live free research page, captures title/publisher/publication date/source hash, extracts methodology fields and stores a source snapshot. The issue links to the rendered evidence record.

## Conflict slice

Two real Reddit/ChatGPT studies appear under the same topic with `contradicts/contextualises` relationships. The summary states why the figures are not directly comparable and confidence is reduced until resolved.

## Executive slice

Given all current events, the generated brief contains no more than five items and may contain zero. A low-significance model release is excluded. A material retrieval/citation change is included with action and confidence.

## POV slice

A source-backed change modifies one specific POV proposition and appends a changelog entry containing old statement, new statement, rationale and evidence IDs. Unrelated POV text remains unchanged.
