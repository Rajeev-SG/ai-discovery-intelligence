ChatGPT

Answers questions from its own crawl of the web plus third-party search providers, links to the pages it uses, recommends products in the US, and now sells ads beneath its answers. Getting in is mostly a robots.txt decision; getting chosen depends on your content.

Sources checked 17 Nov 2026

What to do

  1. 1
    Allow OAI-SearchBot in robots.txtIt is the only way into ChatGPT’s search answers. Blocking GPTBot (training) is a separate choice and doesn’t affect this. Changes take about 24 hours.
  2. 2
    Selling products in the US? Send a product feedApply through ChatGPT merchants. Shopify and Etsy catalogues are already connected. No fees; purchases complete on your own site.
  3. 3
    Measure it as its own channelLinks from ChatGPT search carry utm_source=chatgpt.com. Add it to your analytics channel grouping.
  4. 4
    Test ChatGPT Ads if you can buy conversational intentAds appear below answers, labelled Sponsored, and OpenAI says they don’t influence the answer itself. Self-serve Ads Manager is in beta.

How it works

When it searches Not documented by OpenAI.
Where results come from OpenAI’s own crawler (OAI-SearchBot) plus third-party search providers, which OpenAI doesn’t name. Some news publishers supply content directly through partnerships.

Keeping a page out A disallowed URL can still appear as a link and title if OpenAI learns of it elsewhere. Use a noindex tag — and let the crawler fetch the page so it can see the tag.
Shopping US only for now. Products come from merchant feeds or crawled pages; feeds give more control over how items appear. OpenAI has scaled back its in-chat Instant Checkout — shoppers now buy on the merchant’s site. How products are ranked is not documented.
Ads Launched in the US in February 2026 for logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans; paid plans stay ad-free. Expanded to dozens of countries during 2026 — OpenAI’s own posts give different counts (60+ and 89), so check your market.
Measurement Referral links are tagged utm_source=chatgpt.com. Answers that mention you without a click are invisible to analytics.

Recent changes that matter

  • Late 2026Instant Checkout scaled back; merchant-owned checkout preferred. Feed quality now matters more than checkout integration.
  • 2026Ads expanded beyond the US, with a self-serve Ads Manager. ChatGPT is now a paid media channel, not only an organic one.
  • Feb 2026Ads launched in the US. New crawler OAI-AdsBot reviews ad landing pages.
