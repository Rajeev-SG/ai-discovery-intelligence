# Evidence and provenance

Provenance is part of the product contract.

Every factual assertion must be traceable to evidence carrying at least URL, publisher, publication/capture dates, geography, methodology and source type. Preserve sample size and time window whenever the source supplies them.

## Conflict handling

Two reports can disagree without either being “wrong” if they measure different things. Examples:

- web visits vs referral share vs citation share;
- all prompts vs commercial prompts;
- US English vs global multilingual;
- consumer UI vs API;
- one-day snapshot vs monthly panel;
- citations vs retrieved-but-not-cited sources.

The system must represent these dimensions before labeling evidence contradictory.

## Current exemplar: Reddit in ChatGPT

Semrush reported Promptwatch data showing Reddit's ChatGPT citation share falling from roughly 3.8% to 0.5% in mid-August 2026 and explicitly called the observation provisional. Ahrefs' September 2026 monthly study subsequently had Reddit as the largest cited domain in its US ChatGPT dataset at 16.8% mention share.

Correct agency interpretation: a material short-term disruption was observed by one tracker, but available later evidence does not support the broad statement “ChatGPT stopped using Reddit”. The observation plane should show the event, methods, dates and current reconciled status.
