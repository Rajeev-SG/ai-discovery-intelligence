# Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — useful global observation plane

Render the seeded global surface registry as a polished searchable/filterable/expandable table. This creates immediate value before automated ingestion exists.

## Milestone 2 — free evidence acquisition

Ingest official docs plus high-value free research from Similarweb, Peec, Profound, Ahrefs, Semrush, SISTRIX, Adobe, QuestMobile and other registry sources.

## Milestone 3 — evidence-backed claims

Normalize studies and claims, preserve methods/regions/sample sizes, and display them behind the observation rows.

## Milestone 4 — history/conflict/change

Detect changed sources, backfill material historical changes, reconcile conflicts and expose timelines.

## Milestone 5 — executive intelligence

Generate a restrained weekly brief from significance/confidence rules.

## Milestone 6 — living POV

Maintain the agency SEO/AEO/GEO POV and changelog with traceable evidence triggers.

## Milestone 7 — coverage and operations

Expose source health, stale surfaces and evidence gaps; deploy the production split across Oracle/Hetzner.

## Later

- controlled consumer-UI observational probes where terms permit;
- client-specific first-party data connectors (GSC/Bing/GA4/server logs);
- optional paid visibility-provider integrations;
- brand-specific monitoring;
- public/agency distribution surfaces.
