# Free-source catalogue

`config/sources.yaml` is the canonical registry. This page describes what each class contributes.

## Primary product truth

Official help centres, release notes, product blogs, search/crawler documentation and vendor announcements establish what a product claims to do. They are strongest for launch state, crawler policy, market availability and documented retrieval/search behaviour.

## Market importance

Use free Similarweb research, Statcounter, QuestMobile, DataReportal and equivalent public telemetry to estimate which consumer surfaces matter by geography. Do not merge incompatible denominators into one “market share” number; preserve methodology.

## Commercial impact

Adobe Digital Insights, Similarweb referral research, Cloudflare crawler research and other free datasets help answer whether AI discovery is producing meaningful referral, commerce or crawl behaviour.

## Citation/source ecosystem

Peec, Profound, Ahrefs, Semrush, SISTRIX, Scrunch, Evertune and Seer publish useful public studies. These are vendor-produced studies, so capture sample, dates, geography, surfaces, prompt methodology, metric definitions and commercial interest.

## Discovery, not canonical truth

Search Engine Land, Search Engine Roundtable, Search Engine Journal, GDELT and Google News RSS are useful tripwires. Their job is to surface candidate developments. Whenever possible the canonical claim should resolve to the underlying primary/research source.

## Contradiction example

In August 2026 Semrush reported Promptwatch data suggesting Reddit's ChatGPT citation share had collapsed from roughly 3.8% to 0.5%. In September Ahrefs published a different US dataset in which Reddit was the largest cited domain at 16.8% mention share. The system must preserve both because they use different datasets, time windows and metrics. It should classify the story as contested/possibly temporary rather than emit “ChatGPT stopped using Reddit” as fact.
